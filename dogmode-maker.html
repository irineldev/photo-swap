🐶 Dog Mode Maker

100% offline  This page builds a prank bookmark using a photo from your own device. Nothing is uploaded or downloaded — the photo never leaves your computer, and the finished bookmark loads nothing from the internet.

Step 1 — Pick your prank photo

Choose any image from your computer or phone (the dog, an ugly face, anything).

Step 2 — Add the bookmark

On a computer: show the bookmarks bar (Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + B), then drag this button onto it:

🐶 Dog mode

On a phone: copy this code, bookmark any page, edit the bookmark and paste the code as its address (name it dogmode).

Step 3 — Use it

  1. Open an upload site (imgbb, sendspace, etc.).
  2. Click the 🐶 Dog mode bookmark. A green Dog mode ON badge appears.
  3. Pick or take a photo — your prank photo uploads instead.
  4. Reload the page to turn it off.

Want to change the photo later? Just come back to this page, pick a new one, and drag the button again.
